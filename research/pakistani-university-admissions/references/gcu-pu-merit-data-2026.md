### PU Merit Formula (2026)
Aggregate = (Matric% × 0.10) + (Inter% × 0.40) + (Entry Test% × 0.50)

= 8.98 + 34.28 + 36.50 = **79.77%**

### Second Chance / Retake
If PU offers a retake, required score = (TargetAgg - Matric10% - Inter40%) / 0.50
- Target 85% → need 83.48% on test
